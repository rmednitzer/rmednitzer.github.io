# Roman Mednitzer

Senior Systems & Platform Engineer · Vienna, Austria

I like understanding how things work, especially where technologies and disciplines meet.

## About me

I’m Roman, a systems and platform engineer based in Vienna. I work across infrastructure layers, connecting requirements and hands-on integration with reliable operation, troubleshooting and recovery.

## Professional work

I have around ten years of production infrastructure experience across managed services, enterprise infrastructure and a regulated medical-device environment. My work spans Linux, virtualisation, storage, networking and container platforms.

I work with developers, operations, security teams and service owners to clarify requirements, resolve integration problems and make systems maintainable. I value clear responsibilities, reproducible configuration, useful documentation and evidence from actual operation.

### Platforms and integration

Linux systems, virtualisation, storage and networking, including production work with Kubernetes and OpenShift. I focus on how components fit together and behave as an operational system.

### Operations and recovery

Monitoring, incident analysis and controlled change, with backup administration, documented restore tests and rollback planning. Recovery needs to be demonstrated, not assumed.

### Automation and documentation

Selected automation with Ansible and Terraform, alongside runbooks and operational handover. My experience also includes ISMS documentation, business continuity and ISO/IEC 27001 audit preparation.

## Selected projects

These are personal, AI-assisted projects outside employment. I use AI substantially for implementation, testing and documentation; I own requirements, integration decisions, review and operational validation.

### [relay-shell](https://github.com/rmednitzer/relay-shell)

A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for shell commands, SSH and interactive sessions, with operation records and configurable policy. It is open by default, runs with the service account’s authority and is not a sandbox.

[Documentation and limits](https://github.com/rmednitzer/relay-shell/blob/main/README.md).

### [osint-workbench](https://github.com/rmednitzer/osint-workbench)

A self-hostable workbench for collecting public information, preserving evidence and searching it, with optional enrichment and read-only assistant access.

Work in progress, with bounded workflows and a disposable integration-test environment. Full operational qualification remains open.

[Implementation status](https://github.com/rmednitzer/osint-workbench/blob/main/STATUS.md).

### Personal experimentation

My private lab gives me room to explore AI infrastructure and automation, test integrations, and rehearse migrations and recovery. Some deliberately complex experiments examine how AI agents handle execution feedback and long contexts.
