About

I have around ten years of production experience in managed services, enterprise infrastructure and regulated medical-device environments. I work across Linux, virtualisation, storage, networking, container platforms, automation and recovery.

I translate operational and stakeholder needs into technical requirements, clarify ownership and interfaces, and work with developers, security teams, service owners and vendors. I investigate and implement solutions myself when the scope calls for it. When information is missing, I state the unknowns and verify assumptions rather than guess.

I care about reliable operations, concise procedures, change control, verified recovery and improvement after incidents. I prefer hands-on technical ownership and collaboration to line management. I am interested in AI infrastructure, robotics, mission systems, edge and scientific computing.

Outside work I read hard science fiction, listen to industrial and EBM music, and experiment with technology. I have been an IEEE member since 2013.

Selected projects

These are personal, substantially AI-assisted engineering projects. I define scope and requirements, review and integrate implementations, test outcomes and operate the results. They are prototypes and experiments, not claims of professional software-development or ML-research experience.

relay-shell

Apache-2.0 · MCP

An MCP server for local shell and remote SSH operations, including interactive sessions and file transfers. It records operations, classifies risk and supports optional restrictions. It is open by default and does not sandbox commands. The project explores how agents use tools, handle execution feedback and retain operator control.

osint-workbench

Work in progress

A self-hostable reference implementation for collecting, preserving and searching public information, with optional enrichment and read-only assistant access. Core workflows exist within documented limits, but the complete platform is unfinished and integration validation is ongoing. See its status and evidence.

Personal lab

My private lab is where I evaluate infrastructure integrations, automation and AI-assisted engineering. It is a test environment, not a claim of enterprise production qualification.

It includes a self-hosted information-processing system, local inference, virtualisation, Kubernetes and storage. The public osint-workbench project is a smaller reference implementation inspired by this environment.

Routine operations use controllers, scheduled jobs, monitoring and backups; external AI providers are not required to keep the infrastructure running. I use AI primarily to explore changes, investigate failures and prepare repeatable engineering work.
